# Code of conduct

This applies to everything done in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and
email about the project.

## Expected

- Argue about the work, not the person. Disagreement about a change is normal and welcome; say what you
  would do instead and why.
- Assume the other person means well, and say what you need rather than hinting at it.
- Keep to the subject of the issue or pull request you are in.
- When you are shown to be wrong, say so and move on.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, and comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pursuing an argument after the maintainer has decided it.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct privately to the maintainer at **evand.ukss@gmail.com**.

The maintainer decides what happens: a comment may be edited, hidden or deleted, a thread locked, and a
person warned or blocked from the project. The decision ends the matter
([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

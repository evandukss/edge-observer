# Governance

## Who decides

The project has one maintainer, who decides what is merged and what is released. Where contributors
disagree, the maintainer's decision ends the dispute.

## Taking part

Anyone may open an issue or a pull request. Review is the maintainer's: every pull request is reviewed
by the maintainer, who decides whether it merges. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) says how to build,
test and propose a change.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor becomes a maintainer by the maintainer's invitation, after sustained work on the project.
Each maintainer is recorded in the list below.
